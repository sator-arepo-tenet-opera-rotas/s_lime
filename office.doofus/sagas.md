THE QUEST OF IRVINE HAHN
by Roman Castrato Lovepenny

Irvine was a quiet boy. A shy boy. An introverted boy. He
rarely went out, and he never really got the hang of flirting or anything.
But still. He was happy.
Because he had liquor.
Liquor was Irvine's specialty. His hobby. His interest.
His love. His only friend. Liquor.

One day, Irvine woke up(at 3 PM) with a major hangover.
He wandered out of his room in search of booze.
He did not know it, but ahead of him lay a dangerous and epic adventure.
***
As he wandered out of the safety of his room, the first thing he noticed
was the smell. There was a bizarre smell in the air, but he couldn't place what it was.
"RUB MY SAC OR I'LL KILL YOU!!!", came the scream from the other room.
Irvine ignored his parents' foreplay and continued along his path.
But then the cat appeared. A ferocious demon of a cat, this cat had Irvine in its crosshairs.
And it was moving in for the kill.
The cat rushed toward Irvine. He began to feel the adrenaline
rush. Just as the cat reached him and began rubbing itself on his leg,
Irvine kicked the ASS out of it and it went flying through a nearby window.
"That was close", Irvine said, "I'd better be more careful..."
***
Later on his journey, Irvine encountered his last obstacle..the Ogre.
"I WILL SWALLOW YOUR FACE!!", yelled the Ogre as it leapt out of a nearby trash can.
Irvine's heart skipped a beat.
Before him lay a tall, hairy, semi-pudgey man...totally nude.
"YOU MUST PAY THE TOLL!!", said the Ogre, "FOR I AM THE TROLL!!!"
"You look more like an ogre, father", Irvine said.
"FUCK YOU!!", the Ogre/Troll screamed and tried to punch Irvine.
Irvine dodged, laughed, and got kicked in the testicles.
Things were looking bleak for Irvine Hahn.
***
The pain in his groin made him snap back to reality. He saw a nearby coffee pot,
and took advantage of the situation by slinging acidic-hot coffee into the Ogre/Troll/Father's
face.
He screamed in rage and pain, then left.
Finally, Irvine had found the Sacred Fridge of Booze spoken of in legend.
It lay before him, glowing pure gold.
Irvine opened it, expecting a thousand bottles of absinthe, and wine, and guiness, and to a
lesser extent, gin.
But...
***
Irvine screamed in horror as he saw only a drop of wine left in one bottle,
and not even half a bottle of "some cocktail shit".
"NOOOOOO!!!", he cried in pure terror and rage and pain.
So he did the only thing one could do in this kind of situation.
He drank the booze.
And then he took his father's gun.
And then he commited suicide.

END
